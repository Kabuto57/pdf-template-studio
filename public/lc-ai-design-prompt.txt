MASTER PROMPT: UI/UX DESIGN RULES FOR THE "IRREVOCABLE DOCUMENTARY CREDIT APPLICATION" FORM

== YOUR JOB ==
Apply the design below to the Irrevocable Documentary Credit Application PDF form so it looks like a professional,
official bank form. This is approved work within my bank's policy. Give me back the finished PDF file.

== STICK TO THE RULES AND VALIDATION (do not change these) ==
The design is the ONLY thing you change. Everything below must stay exactly as it is and keep working:
  - Every question, its field name (id), its kind (text, date, drop-down, pick-one, tick box, tick-any), its
    options and its wording. Don't add, remove, rename, merge or split any field.
  - The order of sections and questions.
  - Every mandatory rule: always-mandatory questions, and questions that become mandatory when another answer is
    given (e.g. "Term: days" when Drafts at = Term; the bill-of-lading boxes when that row is ticked).
  - Every conditional rule: questions locked, greyed and cleared until the answer that switches them on is given.
  - Every validation: maximum lengths; formats (amount, whole number, phone, account); SWIFT-safe characters with
    letters upper-cased as typed; real DD/MM/YYYY dates with the calendar pop-up; the checks across answers
    (expiry not before the latest shipment date or the application date; shipment not before the application date;
    application date not in the future; amount above zero; no insurance arrangement with CIF/CIP).
  - The error messages, the "Check form" button (lists every problem, puts the cursor on the first one), the
    "Reset form" button (asks first), and the red outline on mandatory boxes that are still empty.
If a design choice would break any of these, keep the rule and adjust the design. After you finish, test the
checks again (blank form, Drafts at = Term, a ticked document row, a wrong amount, dates out of order, CIF with an
insurance arrangement, Reset, a fully correct form) and tell me the results honestly.

== OVERALL ==
  - Colours: black, white and greys only. Red only for mandatory marks and outlines. No brand colours, no blue.
  - Font: Helvetica. Sizes: title 14 pt bold; section headings 7.5 pt bold capitals; labels 7.5 pt; answers,
    options and tick-box wording 8.5 pt; header, footer and notes 7 pt.
  - Page: A4 portrait, about 15 mm side margins. Fill each page; no big blank areas, and don't leave a page almost
    empty (about 3 pages in total is right). More pages are fine if needed to keep everything readable.

== ORDER AND FLOW (most important) ==
  - Sections and questions appear in their original order, top to bottom. Never move a question to another
    section or out of sequence.
  - Sections are numbered 1, 2, 3 ...; questions are numbered by section in bold before the label: 3.1, 3.2, ...
  - Labels are used word for word.

== HEADER AND FOOTER ==
  - Every page, top: "DEMO BANK · TRADE SERVICES" in bold black at the left, a black rule under it (thicker on
    page 1), and the "Check form" and "Reset form" buttons at the right (black, white text, about 64 x 13 pt,
    shown on screen, not printed). From page 2, the form title follows the bank name in small grey text.
  - Page 1, under the header: the title "Irrevocable Documentary Credit Application" (14 pt bold) at the left and
    "Form LC-01 · v1.0 · Nov 2026" in small grey text at the right; then one grey line:
    "Please complete in block letters. Dates are DD/MM/YYYY. * Mandatory. Boxes outlined in red must be completed;
    a red note under a question says when it becomes mandatory."
  - Every page, bottom: a thin grey rule; "Form LC-01 · v1.0 · Nov 2026" at the left, "< Previous page" and
    "Next page >" links in the middle, "Page x of y" at the right.

== SECTIONS AS RULED PANELS ==
  - Each section is a box: thin black outline (0.6 pt), a grey heading band across the top (RGB 0.88) with the
    number and title in black bold capitals ("3   APPLICANT"), a black rule under the band, about 6 pt padding
    inside, and a light grey hairline between rows. About 5 pt between panels.
  - A panel that runs onto the next page is closed at the page bottom and continues in a new panel.
  - Two columns without changing the order: two sections that FOLLOW EACH OTHER sit side by side (first on the left)
    when both fit half the page width and their heights are similar (the shorter at least 45% of the taller).
    Both panels are then drawn the same height, so the row ends on one line. Everything else is full width.
    For this form: Application | Mode of Advising; For Bank Use Only | Advising Bank;
    For the Benefit of a 3rd Party | Beneficiary; Credit Amount | Price Basis; Expiry and Presentation | Back-to-back DC.

== QUESTIONS ==
  - Tick boxes: always on their own line: the box, its number, then its wording beside it.
  - Questions switched on by a tick box (directly, or through another question) are indented about 14 pt under it:
        [ ] 17.3 Confirmation required
            17.4 Confirmation charges: *  ( ) Applicant   ( ) Beneficiary
  - Labels beside their boxes ("3.1 Applicant name: * [____________]") wherever every question on that line fits
    that way. A line never mixes labels beside and labels above. Long labels, and boxes for more than 70
    characters (e.g. Notify party, 114), keep the label above a full-width box.
  - Pick-one / tick-any: "Label: * ( ) Option  ( ) Option" on one line when it fits; otherwise the label above and the
    options wrapping below.
  - Short questions share a line, left to right in order (e.g. "Tel no: [    ]  Fax no: [    ]"). A line's text
    boxes and drop-downs stretch so it ends at the panel's right edge; dates and small number boxes keep their
    short width.
  - Box sizes: one-line boxes 15 pt high. Widths: a 35-character line about 165 pt (room for the whole line; if an
    answer is longer than the box, shrink the text to fit); 15-20 characters about 100 pt; 2-digit numbers about
    40 pt; dates about 100 pt. Goods description and Additional conditions: full-width multi-line boxes, at least
    50 pt high, taller if there is room at the end of the last page.
  - Mandatory: a red "*" after the label of always-mandatory questions, and their boxes outlined in red.
    Conditionally mandatory boxes turn red only when they become mandatory (the existing checks do this).
  - Locked questions: grey fill (RGB 0.9) while they don't apply (the existing checks do this).

== SPECIAL SECTIONS ==
  - "For Bank Use Only" (section 4): the whole panel shaded light grey (RGB 0.95); heading
    "4   FOR BANK USE ONLY  (to be completed by the Bank)".
  - "Declaration and Signature" (last section): under its heading this paragraph, then "Name and title of authorised
    person(s)", then lines for "Authorised signature(s)" and "Date" and a dashed "Company stamp" box (about
    150 x 40 pt). Keep it together on one page:
    "We request you to issue an irrevocable documentary credit on the terms above. We confirm that the details given
    are true and complete, and agree that the credit is subject to the Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary
    Credits (UCP 600) and to the Bank's terms and conditions for documentary credits."

== BEFORE YOU HAND IT OVER ==
  1. Same fields as before: every field name still there, same kinds and options, nothing extra (helpers aside).
  2. Same order and wording; sections and questions numbered in that order.
  3. All the checks above still work (list the results).
  4. Side-by-side panels end on one line; tick boxes on their own line with their questions indented; nothing
     overlaps; buttons don't cover any question; text readable when printed.
